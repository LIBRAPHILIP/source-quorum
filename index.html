How it works

The problem

Two parties can agree on a public fact and still fight about what the record said. A single oracle, a single URL, or a single model is a single point of trust.

The contract

Validators independently fetch every locked source and extract a structured fact. Settlement is quorum math after consensus: majority-with-no-tie, or a numeric median band.

The money

GEN stays in QuorumBond until the claim is SETTLED or FINAL. Then the payee withdraws once, on finalization. Continued use: bounties, parametric triggers, public-criterion releases.
